# Capstone project brief

Complete before inspecting final test outcomes. Record later changes explicitly.

- Project title and chosen track:
- Intended use and why the prediction matters:
- Unit of observation and stable identifier:
- Target definition and annotation or measurement policy:
- Prediction time and inputs available then:
- Data source, version/fingerprint, acquisition procedure and licence:
- Relevant representation gaps, privacy constraints and consequences of errors:
- Audit procedure and exclusion/grouping rules:
- Training/validation/test partition policy and why it matches the intended use:
- Primary metric, its direction and relevant secondary metrics:
- Relevant simple baseline and classical nonlinear comparator, if appropriate:
- Neural candidate and any checkpoint/tokeniser revision and licence:
- Candidate configurations, random seeds, training and compute budget:
- Validation selection rule, tie handling and any refit policy:
- Final test access policy and evidence to save:
- Predeclared error slices and minimum useful sample counts:
- Resource or operational constraints to measure:
- Conditions under which the recommendation would remain inconclusive:
- Expected deliverables and exact reproduction command:

Reproducing a fixed run is a software check. Changing the method after reviewing
its test outcomes requires a suitable new final evaluation for a new performance
claim. Do not silently turn the test set into a validation set.
